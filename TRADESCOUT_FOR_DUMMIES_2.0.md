# TradeScout for Dummies 2.0

## What Is TradeScout?

A smarter way to find and hire contractors, connect with your community, and make decisions you won't regret.

## What Can You Do With TradeScout?

### Find Trustworthy Contractors

Post your project. Get real recommendations from your community.

See contractor portfolios, reviews, and verification badges.

Message contractors directly. Negotiate rates. Get quotes.

No middleman. No platform taking 30%.

### Build Your Network

Connect with neighbors, business owners, and people in your industry.

See who's in your community and what they do.

Join groups around topics you care about.

Real people. Verified profiles. No bots.

### Get Advice From Real Experience

Ask your community about contractors, vendors, or decisions you're facing.

See answers from people who've actually done what you're thinking about.

Learn from their wins and their mistakes.

### Post Your Services or Sell Stuff

If you're a contractor or business owner, find customers directly.

Build your reputation through real work and real reviews.

Get hired because people in your community trust you.

### Manage Your Community (If You're an HOA or Admin)

See what's happening in your community at a glance.

Catch problems before they become drama.

Verify people who interact with your residents.

## Common Questions

**Q: Is TradeScout free?**

A: The core platform is free. You can find contractors, post your services, and connect with your community for free. Premium features may come later, but the basics are free.

**Q: How do I get started?**

A: Sign up, verify your email, fill out your profile. Then start posting what you need or what you offer.

**Q: Who uses TradeScout?**

A: Homeowners. Contractors. HOA boards. Business owners. Anyone who needs to find or hire trustworthy people in their community.

**Q: How does TradeScout verify people?**

A: Background checks, license verification, community reviews, and activity history. The more you use it, the more visible your track record becomes.

**Q: What if someone lies on their profile?**

A: That's why community reviews and ratings matter. Lies get exposed fast when real work happens.

**Q: Can I message contractors directly?**

A: Yes. TradeScout gets out of your way once you've decided to connect.
