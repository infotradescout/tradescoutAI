# JW Stone customer-facing inventory language

Owner direction, September 12, 2026: the Google Drive folder is the information authority. Display what is supplied; leave missing facts unknown. Source-validation terminology is not customer-facing product information.

## Public copy

- Show material names, slab counts, dimensions, finishes, photos, prices when authorized, and relevant dates using ordinary product labels.
- Use `Slab count: 8`, not `8 slabs recorded in supplied source`.
- Use `Finish`, not `Known finish` or `Confirmed finishes where listed`.
- Use `Slab counts`, not `Source counts where listed`.
- Use an actual `Updated [date]` when the arrival record supplies a valid date. An invalid date must not become a claim that inventory was recently checked.
- Describe New Arrivals in terms of slab selection, quantities, finishes, and pickup or delivery rather than internal physical-lot verification procedures.
- Source IDs, evidence provenance, verification states and operational controls remain internal. Keep meaningful requests to check current availability; a catalog count alone is not a reservation or a live stock promise.

## Unchanged boundaries

This is a presentation change, not a new stock ledger or a Drive synchronization change. Do not rewrite supplied quantities, material identities, media, dimensions, prices, or dates to improve the copy. Missing counts are not zero stock. Missing dimensions and finishes are not inferred. Preserve membership-only private pricing, staff-only landed cost, inventory publication gates, and the existing request flow.

Keep quarry-origin storytelling separate from internal data-source terminology. The instruction does not prohibit the business description `selected at the source`.

Existing persisted legacy profile blocks, operations screens, and broader website copy are not silently rewritten by this change. Any follow-up should inspect the actual consumer and preserve its data ownership rather than editing applied migrations.
