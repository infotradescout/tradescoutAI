# TradeScout free systems and hosted surfaces

TradeScout — Connection Without Compromise.

## Product boundaries

- The original TradeScout site's 70+ free tools are its historical free utility layer. Keep them available and free. Do not derive membership in that set from today's feature inventory or assign a later system to it simply because the system is free.
- Named TradeScout systems can also be shared free tools for users. Exchange, Direct Connect, StoneBid, planners, and other eligible systems retain their own workflows and backend rules. Their free use does not change their origin or ownership.
- Separate product brands and customer records remain separate. Cross-system links and integrations must use an explicit public or authorized data contract.
- Free public business profiles are useful, searchable identities. Custom profile design, hosted business sites, and optional domain service are the intended leading paid layer. Payment cannot purchase organic placement, trust, contact access, or an outcome.

## Connection rule

A hosted page presents the next action only when the visitor's current task and public business data support it. A published listing can link to its exact Exchange detail. A business profile can offer a protected Direct Connect request. A service or project can carry its scope into a relevant tool when that tool accepts it. Each system remains responsible for eligibility, consent, pricing, fulfillment, and record changes. The profile must not duplicate those decisions or send a request on a visitor's behalf.

Use inline links, item cards, and task-specific actions near the content that gives them meaning. Do not add generic cross-promotion banners, forced onboarding detours, or unconditional tool grids to hosted business pages.

## Public identity and discovery

An eligible public surface needs one stable canonical URL, factual public content, internal links from relevant profiles and directories, structured data where the facts support it, and inclusion in the appropriate sitemap. TradeScout-hosted subdomains and customer domains must resolve to the same published identity and retain the platform's visibility and trust checks. A new host requires verified DNS and TLS before activation.

Public projections may include approved listing titles and detail URLs. Private seller IDs, fabricator discounts, supplier-private pricing, buyer contact information, and unpublished inventory stay behind their owning systems' access rules. JW Stone keeps its original $2,500 core profile and its own inventory and pricing authority; later TradeScout systems remain TradeScout-controlled.
