# Steel Home TradePartners gap-coverage pipeline

Checked: 2026-08-11

Internal sourcing research only. Inclusion means the company publicly describes a relevant program; it does not mean TradeScout has been accepted, has negotiated terms, or may use the company's name in public marketing.

## Decision rule

The desired operating model is **TradeScout owns the customer conversation and coordinates the outside company**. Evaluate every opportunity in this order:

1. White-label or co-branded program with TradeScout-controlled support.
2. Dealer, reseller, distributor, or dropship account that permits TradeScout to quote and transact with the customer.
3. Builder, trade, or volume account that lets TradeScout procure for the customer.
4. Affiliate or referral program only as a temporary demand test or negotiation lead.

An affiliate link is not complete coverage: it normally sends the customer to someone else's checkout and support flow.

## Commercial controls required before launch

Before moving any gap from research to a TradeScout quote:

- obtain written acceptance and the controlling dealer, reseller, trade, or white-label terms;
- confirm whether TradeScout may set the customer price and collect payment;
- establish the required resale certificate, sales-tax, credit, and invoicing workflow for each operating state;
- document minimum orders, advertised-pricing rules, branding, blind shipping, lead times, freight terms, damaged-goods claims, cancellations, returns, and chargebacks;
- identify the actual manufacturer, warrantor, service administrator, license holder, and installer responsibilities in the written customer scope;
- build an internal escalation owner so the customer can stay with TradeScout when an outside order has a problem;
- keep the category off the public package page until the operational owner approves both the commercial terms and the claim language.

## Recommended outreach order

1. OnPoint for the only clearly advertised white-label gap.
2. MRCOOL and BuildDirect for the clearest open dealer/resell motions.
3. Ferguson Pro, GE Appliances Pro, IDI, and Lamps Plus Pros for broad package procurement.
4. Architectural Designs or Houseplans for plan procurement that stays inside the TradeScout conversation.
5. ProVia and GlobalTranz for negotiated dealer and freight infrastructure.
6. Affiliate programs only where no controlled transaction path is ready and only outside the current public package page.
