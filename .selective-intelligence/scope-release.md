# Scope and Release

The smallest complete loop is a standalone `/jw-stone` route where a visitor immediately sees real supplied catalog selections under the approved `Current Inventory` heading, opens truthful details in one click, optionally uses normal catalog refinements, saves eligible named stones locally, returns to that wishlist, may read proportional Learn about stone topics, and may deliberately hand a safe selection to Direct Connect.

Customer-path guides, buyer-type modes, and recommendation rails are out of scope and removed.

Live availability is never inferred from the catalog or recorded source counts. Merge to `main` requires owner local preview GO on the JW marketplace lane.
