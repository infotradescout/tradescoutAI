TradeScout Website Features – Full Specification

1. User Accounts & Roles
Universal Signup → single portal; users can hold multiple roles.


Roles Supported:


Homeowner


Contractor (licensed)


Service Provider (cleaners, movers, landscapers, etc.)


Helper (odd jobs, gig work, non-licensed labor)


Realtor


Vehicle Dealer/Seller


HOA/Group Member or Admin


Profiles:


Each role gets a separate profile (with cross-links for multi-role users).


Publicly viewable with verification badges.


Verification:


Contractors → license/insurance.


Realtors → MLS/brokerage ID.


Dealers → dealership documents.


Helpers → ID/address verification.


Homeowners → light verification (email, phone, address).


HOA Admins → admin verification for financial tools.



2. Rankings & Leaderboards
Verified-only Feedback: Reviews only from verified users.


Weighted Reviews:


Completed jobs > casual recommendations.


Jobs landed through platform have the highest weight.


Leaderboard Logic:


Top 3 monthly featured for freshness.


Remaining providers ordered by all-time rank.


Trophy System: Bronze → Diamond lifetime badges with visible counts.


Public Viewing: Anyone can see leaderboards; account required to interact (review, recommend, hire).



3. Helpers & Subcontractors
Helpers Section:


Profiles for unlicensed workers.


Task types: yard work, moving help, handyman, cleanup.


Filter to separate helpers from licensed contractors.


Subcontractor Opportunities:


Contractors can toggle “Accepting Subcontractor Work.”


Job postings for helpers/subcontractors.


Applications submitted through platform.


Contractors can expand labor capacity on-demand.



4. Groups & HOAs
Groups:


Open Groups → local clubs, associations.


Private Groups → HOAs, business networks, trade associations.


HOA Management Suite:


Residents join HOA group.


Features:


Fee collection (Stripe-powered, automated).


Budget dashboard → transparent reporting.


Vendor management (approved providers with performance history).


Materials/assets tracking (shared HOA property).


Voting system for budgets, projects, policy changes.


Announcement board for updates.


HOAs act as “super nodes” → onboarding entire neighborhoods at once.



5. Marketplace & Transactions
Contractors & Service Providers:


Cannot boost profiles.


Can promote discounts/items in a daily deal feed (LuckyBucks 2.0).


Realtors:


Free listings.


Boost options (time-based or impression-based).


Optional subscription packages for high-volume users.


Vehicle Dealers/Sellers:


Free listings.


Boost + subscription options for dealer networks.


General Marketplace:


Tools, materials, equipment, local items.


Boosting applies only to posts, not user profiles.


Payments:


Users can pay off-platform or on-platform.


On-platform transactions via Stripe escrow.


~5% platform fee on completed transactions.



6. Affiliate & Growth System
Every User is an Affiliate:


Unique ID generated at signup.


Auto-attached to every shared link.


Commission Structure:


Flat 10% commission standard.


Higher tiers (15–20%) for Scouts/Brand Ambassadors.


Attribution Model:


Last-click → full credit.


First-click Scouts/Ambassadors → small bonus %.


Credits First:


Earnings accumulate as credits usable on boosts or marketplace.


Option to cash-out via Stripe once thresholds are met.


Affiliate Dashboard:


Snapshot → balance, clicks, referrals.


Full view → earnings history, conversion sources.



7. Community Ecosystem
County Hubs:


One per U.S. county (~3,000+).


Each hub has deal feed, leaderboards, groups.


Community Features:


Posts, comments, polls, photos/videos.


Voting system for local issues.


Moderation:


Regional managers oversee structure.


Local moderators elected by the community.


Moderator permissions: post approval, spam removal, featured deals.


Recognition Badges:


Top Helper


Trusted Subcontractor


Top Community Voice



8. Territories & Scalability
Fixed Boundaries: Organized strictly by county.


Unlimited Users Per County: Multiple contractors, helpers, realtors, dealers.


Verification Unlocks Access: Users may activate roles in multiple counties if licensed/verified.


Nationwide Rollout: Scalable to 3,000+ county hubs.



9. Advertising Model
Local Ads (County-Level):


Who: Local contractors, providers, shops, realtors.


Placement: County feeds, HOAs, group hubs.


Format: Boosted deals, sponsored posts, vendor highlights.


Regional Ads (Multi-County/Metro):


Who: Regional contractors, dealer networks, service chains.


Placement: Multi-county feeds, regional groups.


Format: Sponsored professional groups, region-wide deal feeds.


State Ads:


Who: Statewide brands, trade schools, franchises.


Placement: All county hubs in a state, state carousels.


Format: Campaigns, sponsorships, event tie-ins.


Nationwide Ads:


Who: National brands (Home Depot, Lowe’s, Ford, banks).


Placement: Nationwide banners, community-wide campaigns.


Format: Nationwide promos, contests, foundation tie-ins.


Ad Philosophy:


Never impacts rankings.


Always contextual.


Community-first, with affiliate tagging for virality.



10. Charitable Engine
Profit Donation:


10% of all profits split:


50% → Mike Rowe Works Foundation.


50% → Local community projects in counties where revenue originated.


100% of Donations:


All donations split the same way, no overhead.


TradeScout Coffee Company:


100% of proceeds donated to scholarships, trade education, and community projects.


Narrative: Every transaction strengthens local economies and reinvests directly back.
