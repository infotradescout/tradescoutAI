# TradeScout — User-Facing Feature Catalog

_Last updated: 2025-12-07_

## 1) Onboarding & Access
- Email/password auth; invite-based onboarding; login/signup flows
- Multi-role registration (27 user types) with state + county selection
- Profile setup wizard; address verification; protected routes with role-based access

## 2) Profiles & Personalization
- Public/private profiles; profile-as-website with custom color schemes
- Badge visibility toggle; founder/verified/role badges
- Public profile links; profile settings for defaults and preferences; role directory

## 3) Marketplace & Listings
- Home services marketplace (find contractors, request quotes, daily deals, promos, growth packs)
- Listings: property, vehicle, handmade, real estate
- Quote calculator; lead management; request-quote flows; saved ads

## 4) Contractor & Pro Workflows
- Contractor apply/signup, accelerator, promos, board, leads, dashboards (general/simple)
- Trade-specific dashboards (helper, designer/service variants, specialty roles)
- Verification flows (contractor, license, insurance, background check)
- Business owner dashboard; business listing; car dealer/sales; realtor dashboards/tools (CMA, market analysis, appointments)

## 5) Homeowner / HOA / Community Builder
- Homeowner dashboard; HOA dashboard & management tools
- Community builder dashboards, contribution flows, admin reconciliation; county transparency
- Community directory/hub; county directory

## 6) Community & Social
- Community feed/forum; social integrations
- Comments, posts, notifications, moderation center/content moderation demo
- Leaderboard; referral dashboard; groups & group detail

## 7) Messaging & Collaboration
- Chat/messages, conversations, messaging panel; notification center
- Socket.io/WebSocket messaging (prod-enabled, dev-disabled)

## 8) Scheduling, CRM, Operations
- Schedule consultation; application tracker; CRM dashboards
- File management/document handling; onboarding tours; floating help

## 9) Payments & Commerce
- Checkout, payment processing, payment history/success
- Boosts, promotions, ad creator, advanced search, analytics

## 10) Analytics & Admin
- Admin dashboards (general, pricing analytics, listings, users, testing controls, error reports, workspace)
- System settings; platform analytics; API integrations
- PromptAdminPage for AI system-prompt management (hot reload, role-guarded)
- Admin address/professional verification; community-builder admin; attachments

## 11) AI Assistant & Help
- Assistant landing/chat; Story Generator; helper/assistant pages; subtle hints/onboarding help
- System prompt hot-reload with 4-layer knowledge hierarchy (admin cache → local data → web → honest unknown)

## 12) Geography & Data Integrity
- Full US states/territories dropdown + county selection at signup
- Badges (founder/verified/role) and visibility preference

## 13) Compliance & Legal
- Terms, privacy, compliance, background check, insurance verification, license verification
