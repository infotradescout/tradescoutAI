LISA — Live Indexed Signal Adapter
The Live Intelligence Layer of TradeScout

---

**Overview**

LISA is the live intelligence layer of the TradeScout ecosystem.

It captures and organizes real-world signals generated by activity inside the TradeScout marketplace. Every interaction—requests, responses, quotes, and project completions—produces structured data that reflects the actual state of the local market in real time.

Unlike traditional platforms that rely on delayed or inferred datasets, LISA surfaces truth-current intelligence created directly by marketplace activity.

---

**What LISA Captures**

LISA continuously synthesizes several categories of operational signals.

**Marketplace Signals**

Insights into how the market is behaving right now.

Examples include:
- project demand
- service pricing ranges
- market velocity
- geographic demand shifts
- request-to-completion ratios

**Contractor Signals**

Operational indicators about businesses participating in the marketplace.

Examples include:
- active contractor counts
- response times
- project acceptance rates
- completion history
- reliability and trust indicators

**Community Signals**

Real-time patterns emerging from neighborhoods and local engagement.

Examples include:
- trending topics
- community activity spikes
- local project demand clusters
- engagement velocity

---

**The 4D Data Model**

Every signal captured by LISA includes four dimensions:

- **Time**: When the action occurred.
- **Location**: Where the activity took place geographically.
- **Intent**: What the user or business was trying to accomplish.
- **Outcome**: What actually happened as a result.

Together these dimensions produce 4D operational data that reflects real marketplace behavior rather than inferred trends.

This enables analytics and operational systems to operate on live intelligence instead of historical snapshots.

**Architectural Context**

LISA can be understood as three layers within the TradeScout intelligence system.

- **Signal Layer**: Raw activity data generated by marketplace operations.
- **Lens Layer**: Filtered, contextualized views of that data (LISA Lens).
- **Decision Layer**: Insights used by Scout, partners, and analytics systems.

The current documentation focuses primarily on the Signal Layer, which forms the foundation of the system.
